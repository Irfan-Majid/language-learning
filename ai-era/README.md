# AI Era — Technical Skills for Career Security

> AI is not your enemy. **Developers who ship AI-powered products** are among the most in-demand hires in 2026.

This folder teaches you to **build with AI** — the skill that keeps you ahead of layoffs and obsolescence.

## Why Learn This?

| Without AI skills | With AI skills |

| "I hope they don't replace us" | "I built our AI feature" |
| Competing on typing speed | Competing on product thinking |
| One of many coders | The AI integration person |
| Fear in interviews | Confident on new question types |
