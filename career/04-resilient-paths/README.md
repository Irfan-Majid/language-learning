# Resilient Career Paths — Where to Aim

Honest guide to roles that stay strong when AI and layoffs hit the industry.

---

## Path Comparison

| Path | Layoff risk | AI impact | Entry difficulty | Income ceiling |
|------|-------------|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| **Product engineer (full-stack)** | Medium | Augmented by AI | Medium | High |
| **Platform / DevOps / SRE** | Low–Medium | Low (infra is hard) | Medium–High | Very high |
| **Security engineer** | Low | Low | High | Very high |
| **Data engineer** | Low–Medium | Augmented | Medium | High |
| **AI application engineer** | Low (growing) | Central | Medium | Very high |
| **QA / test automation lead** | Medium | Evolving up | Medium | Medium–High |
| **Tech lead / architect** | Low at good companies | Low | High (experience) | Very high |
| **Pure frontend (junior)** | Higher | Higher pressure | Low | Medium |
| **WordPress-only freelancer** | Medium | Medium pressure | Low | Medium |

---

## Path 1: Product Engineer (Recommended for Most)

**What you do:** Own features from idea → code → deploy → measure

**Stack to learn:**
```
languages/typescript/ OR languages/python/
frameworks/react/ OR frameworks/nextjs/
frameworks/fastapi/ OR frameworks/express/
databases/postgresql/
devops/docker/
ai-era/04-llm-apis/   ← add AI feature to stand out
```

**Why resilient:** You deliver business value, not just tickets. AI speeds you up; it doesn't replace ownership.

---

## Path 2: Platform / DevOps Engineer

**What you do:** CI/CD, Kubernetes, monitoring, reliability

**Stack to learn:**
```
languages/go/ OR languages/bash/
devops/docker/
devops/kubernetes/
languages/python/  (automation scripts)
```

**Why resilient:** When apps break at 2 AM, AI doesn't SSH into servers. Cloud spend keeps growing.

---

## Path 3: AI Application Engineer (Fastest Growing)

**What you do:** Build products ON TOP of LLMs — chatbots, RAG, agents, copilots

**Stack to learn:**
```
languages/python/
frameworks/fastapi/
ai-era/  (entire folder)
databases/postgresql/ + redis/
languages/typescript/  (for frontend)
```

**Why resilient:** Every company wants AI features. Few developers can ship them reliably.

**You don't need a PhD.** You need APIs, prompts, data pipelines, and error handling.

---

## Path 4: Enterprise Backend (Java / C# / Go)

**What you do:** APIs, microservices, integrations at banks, insurance, government

**Stack to learn:**
```
languages/java/ + frameworks/spring-boot/
  OR languages/csharp/ + frameworks/aspnet-core/
  OR languages/go/
databases/postgresql/
```

**Why resilient:** Slow to change, long contracts, compliance requirements AI can't shortcut.

---

## Path 5: QA → Test Architect

**What you do:** Test strategy, automation frameworks, CI quality gates

**Stack to learn:**
```
languages/javascript/ OR languages/python/
testing/jest/, testing/cypress/, testing/pytest/
ai-era/06-ai-testing/
Your app's stack 06-testing/
```

**Why resilient:** AI writes code faster → more bugs → more need for structured testing.

---

## Path 6: Tech Lead / Architect (Experience Required)

**What you do:** System design, technical decisions, mentor team

**Requirements:** 5+ years typically, strong communication

**Stack to learn:**
```
Everything in 04-advanced/ and 05-expert/ across 2+ stacks
System design practice
career/07-project-management/ in your stacks
```

**Why resilient:** Judgment and accountability. AI advises; humans decide.

---

## Path 7: Domain Specialist Developer

**What you do:** Developer who deeply knows an industry

| Domain | Example skills |
|--------|----------------|
| **Fintech** | Payments, PCI, ledger systems |
| **Healthcare** | HIPAA, HL7, patient data |
| **E-commerce** | Inventory, checkout, fraud |
| **Logistics** | Routing, warehouses, tracking |

**Why resilient:** AI lacks your industry's rules, politics, and history.

---

## Switching Paths (It's OK)

| From | To | Bridge |
|------|-----|--------|
| Junior frontend | Product engineer | Add backend + SQL |
| Manual QA | Test automation | `testing/` + JavaScript |
| PHP developer | AI application eng | Python + `ai-era/` |
| Any developer | DevOps | Docker → K8s → Go |
| Backend only | Full-stack | React or Vue fundamentals |

Use `08-stack-switching/` in each technology folder.

---

## What NOT to Do

| Trap | Why |
|------|-----|
| Learn 15 frameworks shallowly | No depth = easy to replace |
| Ignore AI completely | Market expects AI fluency |
| Only leetcode, no projects | Interviews ≠ employment |
| Stay in dead technology 5+ years with no plan | Risk compounds |
| Wait until layoff to network | Too late for warm referrals |

## Choose Your Path (Exercise)

Answer honestly:

1. What do I enjoy? (building UI / APIs / infra / data / testing)
2. What does my local job market hire? (check LinkedIn jobs this week)
3. What's my timeline? (urgent job need vs long-term growth)
