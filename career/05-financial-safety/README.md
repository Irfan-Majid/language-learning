# Financial Safety Net for Developers

Money in the bank is **career security** — it lets you say no to bad jobs and survive layoffs without panic.

---

## Target Numbers

| Fund | Target | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Emergency fund** | 6 months of expenses | Layoff buffer |
| **Job search fund** | +$500–2000 | Interview clothes, courses, certs |
| **Health buffer** | Know your insurance gap | COBRA or local equivalent costs |

## If You're Starting From Zero

| Month | Action | Target |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Track every expense | Know your number |
| 2 | Cut 1–2 subscriptions | Save $20–100/month |
| 3 | Auto-transfer on payday | Even $50 counts |
| 4 | Side income (see below) | Boost savings rate |
| 5–6 | Build to 1 month | First milestone |
| 7–12 | Build to 3 months | Serious buffer |
| 13–18 | Build to 6 months | Layoff-ready |

**$50/month for 5 years = $3,000 + interest.** Better than $0.

## Side Income Options (Developers)

| Option | Effort | Income potential |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Freelance small projects** | Medium | $500–5000/project |
| **Upwork/Fiverr automation scripts** | Low–Medium | Variable |
| **Tutoring / mentoring** | Low | $20–80/hour |
| **Technical writing** | Medium | $100–500/article |
| **Build small SaaS tool** | High | Passive potential |
| **Open source sponsorship** | Low | Small but builds brand |

**While employed:** Side income → savings only, not lifestyle inflation.

## Salary Negotiation (Keep More of What You Earn)

| Rule | Action |
|------|--------|
| Always research range | levels.fyi, Glassdoor, local recruiters |
| Never share old salary first | Deflect: "Looking for market rate for this role" |
| Negotiate offer | 10–20% bumps are common |
| Ask for signing bonus | Especially if base is fixed |
| Review annually | Inflation + skills growth = raise request |

**10% raise on $80k = $8,000/year.** One conversation.

## Layoff Financial Checklist

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Severance | Understand weeks offered vs tenure |
| Unused PTO | Some states/countries pay out |
| Stock options | Vesting schedule, exercise window |
| 401k / pension | Don't cash out early — penalties |
| Health insurance | COBRA deadline: often 60 days to elect |
| Unemployment | File day 1 if eligible |
| Expense freeze | 90-day minimum spending cut |

---

## Geographic Arbitrage (If Possible)

| Strategy | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Remote for high-cost-of-living salary | Live in lower COL area |
| Contract internationally | USD/EUR rates vs local currency |
| Relocate for tech hubs | Higher salary, higher rent — calculate net |

---

## Debt Priority

| Priority | Type | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| 1 | High-interest credit card | 20%+ APR destroys savings |
| 2 | Emergency fund to 1 month | Before aggressive debt pay |
| 3 | Emergency fund to 6 months | Before investing heavily |
| 4 | Student loans / mortgage | Lower urgency if rate < 5% |

---

## Mental Health & Money

Financial stress makes layoff fear worse. Small wins help:

- [ ] Know your exact monthly number (removes unknown fear)
- [ ] Set up automatic savings (removes willpower burden)
- [ ] Celebrate milestones (1 month, 3 months saved)
- [ ] Talk to partner/family about plan (alignment reduces conflict)
