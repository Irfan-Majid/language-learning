# Networking — Your Hidden Job Security

**Most jobs are filled through referrals**, not job boards. Network = options before you need them.

---

## Why Network When You Have a Job?

| Stat (industry average) | Reality |
|-------------------------|---------|
| 70%+ roles never public | Hidden market |
| Referred candidates 4× more likely hired | Trust transfer |
| Layoff recovery via referral | Weeks, not months |

Networking when employed feels less desperate and builds genuine relationships.

---

## Start Small (Introvert-Friendly)

| Action | Time | Frequency |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Comment thoughtfully on 1 LinkedIn post | 5 min | 2×/week |
| Connect with 1 person (personal note) | 5 min | 1×/week |
| Share 1 thing you learned | 15 min | 1×/month |
| DM 1 former colleague "how are you?" | 5 min | 1×/month |
| Join 1 Discord/Slack community | 30 min | 1×/week lurk |

**You don't need to be extroverted.** Consistency beats charisma.

---

## Connection Request Template

```
Hi [Name], I saw your post about [specific topic] —
really resonated with my work on [related thing].
I'm a [role] working with [stack]. Would love to
connect and learn from your posts.
```

**Not:** "Hi, I'd like to add you to my professional network."

---

## Build Your List (Target: 100 Meaningful Contacts)

| Category | Target count | Who |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| Former colleagues | 20 | People who saw your work |
| Same-stack developers | 30 | Local or online community |
| Recruiters (specialized) | 10 | Your stack, not random |
| Tech leads / seniors | 15 | Learn from, maybe mentor |
| Alumni / school / bootcamp | 15 | Shared background |
| Conference / meetup | 20 | Even virtual events |

Track in spreadsheet: Name, Company, Stack, Last contact, Notes.

---

## When You're Looking (Quietly or Openly)

### Quiet search (still employed)
```
Hi [Name], hope you're well. I'm exploring new opportunities
in [stack/area] and remembered your work at [company].
Would you have 15 minutes for a confidential chat about
the market? No pressure if timing is bad.
```

### After layoff
```
Hi [Name], I wanted to let you know my role at [company]
was eliminated as part of a restructuring. I'm now looking
for [role type] opportunities, ideally [stack/domain].
If you hear of anything or know someone I should talk to,
I'd really appreciate an intro. Happy to share my resume.
```

## Give Before You Ask

| Give | Ask (later) |
|------|-------------|
| Answer someone's question | Referral when job searching |
| Share useful article | Intro to hiring manager |
| Review someone's PR | Feedback on your resume |
| Intro two people who should meet | Coffee chat about their company |

---

## Recruiter Strategy

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Connect with 5–10 specialized recruiters | Blast 100 generic messages |
| Respond even if "not now" | Ghost — they remember |
| Update them when situation changes | Lie about other offers |
| Know your salary range | Say "flexible" with no number |

| 4 | Follow up with 2 dormant contacts |
