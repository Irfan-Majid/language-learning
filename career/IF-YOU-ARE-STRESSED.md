# If You Are Stressed About AI and Your Job

Read this when anxiety is high. Come back to the plans when you're ready to act.

---

## First: What Is True Right Now

1. **Your fear is valid.** The industry is noisy. Headlines are scary. That does not mean *you* are doomed.

2. **AI is a tool, not your replacement** — if you use it and own outcomes. The risk is staying the same while the job market changes.

3. **Layoffs are usually about money and structure**, not one person being "worse than AI." Whole teams get cut while other companies hire.

4. **Developers who prepare recover.** Skills + proof + people + savings work. Panic without action does not.

5. **You have time.** Not infinite time — but enough to start today with one small step.

---

## What NOT to Do When Anxious

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Doom-scrolling layoff news for hours | Increases fear, adds zero skill |
| Learning 10 frameworks in 1 week | Shallow panic learning |
| Deciding "tech is over" and quitting | Extreme reaction to headlines |
| Comparing yourself to Twitter influencers | Curated success, not reality |
| Doing nothing because it's overwhelming | Small steps compound |

## Roles That Need YOU (Not Just AI)

- Someone who **owns** the feature when it breaks
- Someone who talks to **PMs and users**
- Someone who **deploys** and **monitors** production
- Someone who **reviews** AI output before customers see it
- Someone who knows **why** the business needs this code
- Someone who **mentors** juniors
- Someone who says **no** to unsafe AI use

That can be you. It gets easier with deliberate practice.
