## Key Concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| KPI | Key number executives watch (revenue, churn %) |
| Dimension | Category (region, product, time) |
| Measure | Number you aggregate (sum of sales) |
| Drill-down | Click summary → see details |

---

## Power BI vs Excel

| Excel | Power BI |
|-------|----------|
| Small data, ad-hoc | Large data, recurring reports |
| Everyone knows it | Dashboards refresh automatically |
