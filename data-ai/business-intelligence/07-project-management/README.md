# Project Management - Business Intelligence

**Audience:** Project Management specialists working on Business Intelligence projects

---

## Overview

AI project risks: data quality, model drift, cost overruns. Typical timeline: POC 2 weeks, MVP 6-8 weeks. Stakeholder communication for non-technical audiences.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Understand Business Intelligence deliverables
- [ ] Know test/evaluation criteria for this domain
- [ ] Coordinate with data engineering and product
- [ ] Document assumptions and limitations
