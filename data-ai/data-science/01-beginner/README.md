## The Data Science Process

1. **Ask** — What business question?
2. **Get** — SQL, API, CSV
3. **Clean** — Missing values, types
4. **Explore** — Charts, correlations
5. **Model** — ML (later)
6. **Tell** — Present to non-technical people
